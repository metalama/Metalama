# Appendix B. Weak spots

> Part of the [call-site interceptors design](README.md). Previous: [appendix-a-evidence.md](appendix-a-evidence.md) | Next: [appendix-c-review-log.md](appendix-c-review-log.md). Evidence prefixes and terms: [00-conventions.md](00-conventions.md).

## Appendix B. Weak spots that remain after the review

1. Aspect references inside interceptor templates (for example `meta.This.Member` through invokers): the ordering index `int.MaxValue - 1` of the registering layer resolves them to the state after that layer, not after the whole stage (section [10.4.7](10a-oss-bridge-hook-factory.md#1047-attribution-and-ordering)). The expected semantics for interceptors registered by early aspects is not proven.
2. `MethodBuilder` with `MethodKind.LocalFunction` as `meta.Target` (section [10.6.4](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1064-expansion), RC26), and the temporary builder of `ExtensionTemplateServices.CanImplement` (section [10.6.1](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1061-selection-at-declaration-time)): other engine code may assume that builders are type members of a transformation.
3. The synthetic aspect instance that the factory creates per stage for project and namespace fabric origins (sections [10.2.3](10a-oss-bridge-hook-factory.md#1023-contribution-origin) and [10.4.7](10a-oss-bridge-hook-factory.md#1047-attribution-and-ordering)): its registration among the ordered layers, its pipeline step index and its behavior in `AspectReferenceResolver` were not verified in code.
4. Change S1 (section [10.8.5](10c-oss-reference-graph-design-time.md#1085-change-s1-multi-stage-design-time-accumulation)) is required but designed only at the level of its effect: the accumulation of design-time contributors across stages, and the new stage-index parameter of `ExecuteDesignTimePipelineContributorsAsync`.
5. The hierarchical options helper of section [10.8.6](10c-oss-reference-graph-design-time.md#1086-hierarchical-options-at-design-time) is new. Whether the option sources of the configuration can be applied to an analyzer compilation without running aspects was not verified.
6. Runtime async (section [7.8](07-await-interception.md#78-semantics-preserved-and-changed-by-mode-await)): the resumption analysis is proven for the classic continuation code; the runtime-async inlining rule was checked, but other runtime-async code paths were not.
7. `ReceiverVariableClassifier` (section [6.2.6](06a-call-site-model.md#626-variable-classification-without-internal-roslyn-apis)) replicates internal Roslyn rules with public APIs, now including member-binding receivers of conditional accesses. A missed case gives a compile error (wrong `Ref`) or silently lost mutations (wrong `Value`).
8. The limitation `ReceiverReassignedByArguments` (section [6.2.3](06a-call-site-model.md#623-limitations)) needs a conservative analysis of assignments, `ref` arguments and captures of the receiver variable. Its precision in real code was not measured.
9. The `ScopedParameter` limitation now covers every `Ref` or `In` receiver that involves a ref-like type. Its frequency in span-heavy code, and therefore the number of LAMA1012 warnings, is unknown while `FactorySupportsScoped` stays false.
10. The detection of an unused call value, which decides whether `ResultCast` is emitted (section [10.5.6](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1056-syntax-of-the-rewritten-call)), lists the known statement contexts. Other contexts where C# discards a value were not enumerated exhaustively.
11. Omitted type arguments with anonymous types (section [6.4.6](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#646-generic-specialization-and-lifting)): the soundness argument was not tested against complex generic methods.
12. Design-time filing (section [9.7.2](09-premium-engine.md#972-filing-key)): namespace-scope static fabrics in partial runs, and partial types spread over several files after an aspect is removed.
13. Check C5 (section [6.5.2](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#652-checks)) runs one lookup per call site and per candidate name. Its cost for groups with many conditional-access call sites was not measured, and the visibility of the internal classes of friend assemblies through `LookupSymbols` was not verified on a two-project build.
14. The nullable context of synthesized members (section [10.6.4](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1064-expansion)): the detection of the project default for a new tree, and the interaction with the formatter and with the existing nullable directives of the file, were not verified.
15. The speculative binding of an extension `GetAwaiter` at the primary declaration part of a type placement (section [7.10.2](07-await-interception.md#7102-placements)) was not verified.
16. Line numbers of the linker and of the premium engine come mostly from 2026.1, with offsets stated for 2027.0 but not verified line by line.
17. `GetInterceptorMethod` in the Roslyn 5.0.0 engine variant was not verified.
18. `IsSourceStage` compares partial compilations (section [10.3.2](10a-oss-bridge-hook-factory.md#1032-the-context)), and the engine asserts that stage 0 is the source stage. A future change to the order of the system aspect layers would break the assertion.
19. The cost model and the budgets of section [12.15](12-test-plan.md#1215-performance-benchmark) are not measured.
20. The schedule estimates of section [11.5](11-delivery-plan.md#115-mapping-to-release-trains) are rough, and they depend on the C# 15 work of 2027.0 in the same linker files (risk T27).
21. The at-most-once proceed check (section [10.6.5](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1065-proceed-multiplicity)) counts emitted expressions; direct awaits of `meta.Target.Parameters[0]` are not detected.
22. The preview shows calls to the generated static class without its declarations (section [9.8](09-premium-engine.md#98-preview-live-templates-and-introspection)).
23. Three API findings (API-06, API-10 and API-17 of [Appendix C](appendix-c-review-log.md#appendix-c-review-log)) are deferred to the API usability review before M2. The public surface may still shrink. API-11 is resolved (see the end of this appendix).
24. The merge of name filters per reference kind (section [10.7.5](10c-oss-reference-graph-design-time.md#1075-shared-index-of-source-references)) changes which references the Validation engine receives once it reads the shared index. The effect on existing validator baselines was not measured; F20 is the gate.
25. The shared binding relies on every consumer using the same `SemanticModelProvider` of the source compilation, and on Roslyn caching member models per semantic model. The saving was not measured (benchmark B7).
26. Resolved by the third product-owner batch: the result-level record is renamed `AwaitRewriteOptions` (RC53).
27. Condition R2a (section [6.4.1](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#641-receiver-mapping)) needs a position inside the body of an instance member of the placement. When none exists, the structural fallback is conservative and can refuse R2 where the speculative binding would accept it. Its behavior with partial types spread over several files, with types introduced by aspects, and with generic placements was not verified.
28. The local-function stand-in of E16 (section [6.6](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#66-signature-validation-existing-methods-and-adjusted-signatures-r9)) does not exist at call sites in initializers, constructor initializers and primary-constructor base arguments, and it cannot reproduce the extension form of R1x, because a local function cannot be an extension method. In these cases, an adjusted signature that does not bind is reported by the compiler on the generated code instead of LAMA1013. The stand-in binds a conditional-access call site in the static form, which has the same applicability but was not verified for every receiver kind.
29. Method-reference sites (section [6.4.12](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#6412-method-reference-sites)): the exception types thrown for a null receiver by the original conversion, by an R2 method group and by the wrapper were derived from the code generation of Roslyn, not measured. The runtime tests of section [12.8](12-test-plan.md#128-premium-runtime-execution-tests-runtime) fix them.
30. The attribute policy of method-group convertible signatures (section [6.4.5](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#645-attributes)) was designed from the known behavior of ASP.NET Core minimal APIs, without a verification in the ASP.NET Core source. Other reflection-based frameworks may read attributes that the deny list removes, or be disturbed by attributes that it keeps.
31. Rule E18 (section [6.6](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#66-signature-validation-existing-methods-and-adjusted-signatures-r9)) binds `var d = X.E;` speculatively for sites whose converted type is an anonymous delegate type. The equality of two anonymous delegate types obtained from two semantic models was not verified.
32. The proof of concept of section [12.5](12-test-plan.md#125-in-repository-proof-of-concept-of-interceptors) is sized from the earlier test-only extension. Its size and the number of its tests are estimates.
33. Positions of the expression forms of accessor sites (section [6.2.11](06a-call-site-model.md#6211-accessor-sites), [6.4.13](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#6413-accessor-sites)). The pattern-variable forms were compiled in the positions listed in section [6.2.11](06a-call-site-model.md#6211-accessor-sites), with one compiler version. Positions not in the list, such as `switch` expression arms, `when` clauses, interpolation holes and collection-expression elements, were not compiled, and the discard form of `for` incrementors relies on a lookup of `_` that was not prototyped.
34. Single evaluation at compound sites. The rule of section [6.2.11](06a-call-site-model.md#6211-accessor-sites), step 8, that decides when a receiver needs no temporary, is conservative for locals and parameters, but its analysis of assignments, `ref` arguments and captures in the right operand was not measured on real code. A missed case evaluates the receiver twice.
35. Existing setters. The admissibility of a `void` setter depends on `IsResultUsed`, which uses the statement contexts of section [10.5.6](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1056-syntax-of-the-rewritten-call) (see item 10). A context that discards a value and is not listed makes a `void` setter look inadmissible, which is safe, and the reverse would not compile.
36. The proceed of the setter of a C# 14 extension property emits the call to the implementation method as a statement followed by the value (section [10.4.4](10a-oss-bridge-hook-factory.md#1044-synthesized-methods-and-proceed-bindings)). The template compiler support for this expansion of `return meta.Proceed();` was not verified.
37. The one-source rule of section [9.5.8](09-premium-engine.md#958-conflict-detection-r7-b7) now counts owners instead of registrations. The earlier test `SameAspect_WithAndRoot_SameTarget_Error` expected LAMA1010 for two registrations of one aspect on one target, and it now expects one interception. A registration that an aspect makes twice by mistake is therefore no longer reported.
38. `BaseMostAccessibleType()` (section [6.5.6](06b-signatures-and-validation.md#656-base-most-accessible-type)). A template that uses a member of the calling type through `meta.This` fails only when the C# compiler compiles the generated code, with an error such as CS1061 at the placement. No check before expansion can detect it, because member accesses on `meta.This` are run-time code that the template compiler emits without binding it.
39. The preview of a site whose `BaseMostAccessibleType()` placement is declared in another file (section [9.8](09-premium-engine.md#98-preview-live-templates-and-introspection)). Whether the tree of a base type is part of the partial compilation of the preview, and therefore whether the site receives LAMA1030 or is only left unchanged by the injection step, was not verified.
40. `async` await templates (section [7.9.1](07-await-interception.md#791-accepted-template-shapes)). No existing test declares an `async ValueTask<dynamic?>` template. The binding of such a template to a synthesized method, and the expansion of `await meta.Proceed()` in an `async` template, are designed from the code of `TemplateBindingHelper` and `ProceedHelper` and were not run.
41. The lightweight `IMethod` of stage 1, `InterceptorSignatureMethod` (section [5.6.8](05b-api-providers-contexts-results.md#568-parameter-binding-and-the-signature-builder)). The builder must behave as a method of the code model before the `MethodBuilder` of the factory exists. Code of a `configure` function can call members that such a method cannot support, for example invokers or `ToRef()` on a method that no compilation contains. The supported members and the exceptions of the others were not designed member by member.
42. Identity of binders (section [5.6.8](05b-api-providers-contexts-results.md#568-parameter-binding-and-the-signature-builder)). A binder of an existing method is a per-site wrapper whose `ToRef()` and declaration comparison give the wrapped method, while reference equality is `false`. User code that compares the binder with `==` to a method of the code model, or that uses it as a dictionary key, sees two objects. The behavior of the declaration comparer on the wrapper was not verified.
43. Positions of the temporaries of reordered arguments (section [5.6.8](05b-api-providers-contexts-results.md#568-parameter-binding-and-the-signature-builder)). The statement block and the `is var` forms reuse the forms of compound accessor sites, which were compiled only in the positions of section [6.2.11](06a-call-site-model.md#6211-accessor-sites) (item 33). A reordered call in a `switch` expression arm, a `when` clause, an interpolation hole or a collection-expression element, and a pattern variable captured by a lambda, as in the sketch of section [16.8](16-future-directions.md#168-the-proceed-and-packedarguments-sources-interception-without-a-template), were not compiled.
44. `AnyAwaitable` (section [10.6.9](10b-oss-linker-and-templates.md#1069-anyawaitable)). The availability of `AsyncMethodBuilderAttribute` in the netstandard2.0 build of `Metalama.Framework`, the classification of `AnyAwaitable` by the template compiler, and the acceptance of `async AnyAwaitable<dynamic?>` by the template binding were designed from the code and were not run.
45. Packed `params` arguments (section [5.6.9](05b-api-providers-contexts-results.md#569-added-parameters-and-pulled-values)). The statement that a collection expression allocates as the expanded form of the compiler, including inline arrays for `params ReadOnlySpan<T>` and `Array.Empty<T>()` for an empty array, is a requirement that the test `Invocation_PackedParams_SameAllocation` must confirm; it was not measured.
46. The pull guard (section [5.6.9](05b-api-providers-contexts-results.md#569-added-parameters-and-pulled-values)) confirms the binding of the name with a speculative binding at the site. Its behavior in a query clause, whose range variables are lambda parameters after lowering, was not verified.

API-11 of [Appendix C](appendix-c-review-log.md#appendix-c-review-log) (`InterceptorBinding` and the `IntroductionScope` convention) is resolved: `InterceptorBinding` was removed by the follow-up product-owner review ([Appendix D](appendix-d-product-owner-reviews.md#appendix-d-product-owner-reviews-of-2026-09-24-and-2026-09-25), D-I).
