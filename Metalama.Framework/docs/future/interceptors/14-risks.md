## 14. Risk register

Likelihood and impact use Low, Medium and High.

| Id | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Section |

| T1 | The syntactic rewrite changes semantics: evaluation order, struct receivers, caller information, named and optional arguments, `params`, interpolated-string handlers. | High | High | Derivation from `IOperation`; rules table; runtime tests with a control path; corpus test; unknown shapes are declined. | 6, 12.8, 12.9 |
| T2 | Await semantics change: resumption context, single await of a `ValueTask`, `AsyncLocal`, stack traces, allocations. | T3 | Call sites are lost when the linker moves, inlines or copies bodies (promoted fields, templates that embed source expressions). | Low | High | The rewrite happens in the injection step, before any move, copy or inlining (RC39); the rewriter descends into declarators, base lists and top-level statements; fix F15; the completeness check of the rewriter (LAMA0660); the limitation `PromotedFieldInitializer`. | 10.5 |
| T4 | Build-time cost of binding: awaits bind every body in scope that contains an await; validators and interceptors bind the same bodies twice until F20 migrates the Validation engine to the shared index. | Medium | Medium | Early exit; mandatory names in member registrations; the shared index of source references with one binding per stage; merge of name filters per reference kind; benchmark budgets, including B7. | 9.2.3, 9.6, 10.7.5, 12.15 |
