| PO52 | Target selection. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC46). | PO57 | Terminology. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC54 to RC57). The declaration that receives a synthesized interceptor is its placement: `InterceptorPlacement` replaces `InterceptorContainer`, with the same factories, and the related members follow. A registration has a scope and a target selection. A site has an origin and a destination, with the meaning of `ReferenceEndRole.Origin` and `ReferenceEndRole.Destination`: `InterceptionContext.Origin` (`IDeclaration`) replaces `CallingDeclaration` and `CallingMember`, `MethodInterceptionContext.Destination` (`IMember`) replaces `InterceptedMember`, `AwaitableType` is the destination of an await site, and the meta extensions follow. The members use plain code-model types, not `ReferenceEnd`. The direction words inbound and outbound are not used. | PO58 | Target selection of await registrations. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC58). `InterceptAwaits( <provider form>, AwaitInterceptionOptions? options = null )` on every surface, with no awaited type, no type predicate and no filter by awaitable kind. `AwaitInterceptionOptions` keeps only `Scope`. `AwaitableKinds`, `Kinds` and `LookThroughConfigureAwait` are removed, and the kind of one await is the non-flags `AwaitableKind`. | PO59 | Result type of await interceptors. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC59). The awaitable type of the interceptor may differ from the original. The resumption rules are unchanged. | Requiring the identical result type refuses sound interceptors, such as `ValueTask<string>` for an await of `Task<object>`, and treats existing methods and templates differently. | M5 |
| PO60 | Templates of await interceptors. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC60). A template name, as a `string` or a `TemplateInvocation`, never a `MethodTemplateSelector`; `InterceptorResult.Template( string, ... )` is added, and a selector with an alternative template at an await site is reported with LAMA1014. An `async` template awaits `meta.Proceed()`, which returns the raw awaitable, and its declared task type selects the task type of the interceptor. A non-async template receives the awaited value, and the interceptor is `async` with the default task type, which `AwaitRewriteOptions.TaskKind` overrides. Mode `Awaitable` requires a non-async template. PO66 adds `async AnyAwaitable<dynamic?>`, with which the engine chooses the task type, and keeps the literal meaning of the declared task types. | A selector has no target method whose shape could select a variant at an await site, and the `async` modifier of the template already carries the information. | M5 |
| PO61 | Base-most accessible placement. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC61). `InterceptorPlacement.BaseMostAccessibleType()`: the base-most source type of the calling hierarchy that passes every check of `InType`, found by a walk that stops at the first failing type, with the calling type as the fallback. Sibling types share one method. `meta.This` has the type of the selected base type. No new placement code; errors of templates that need members of the calling type are reported by the C# compiler. | Users name the shared base type with `InType( baseType )`, which requires them to know the hierarchy and fails with LAMA1015 when a site cannot use it. | M3 |
| PO62 | Parameter binding API. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC62, RC63, RC69). `InterceptorArgument` with the sources `Receiver`, `Argument`, `Value`, `Awaitable`, `CallerInstance`, `CallerInfo` and `Pull( PullAction )` (without `IntroduceParameterAndPull`); `IInterceptorMethodBinder`, passed to `InterceptorResult.ExistingMethod( method, bind )`; `IInterceptorBuilder`, derived from it and passed to `configure`, which replaces `IMethodInterceptorBuilder`; separate parameter-list types for the binder and the builder. Binders and builders are valid only during the callback. `CallerInstance` is in version 1. The arguments of the site are evaluated once, left to right, with temporaries when a binding reorders them. Bindings are not part of the key. At a method-reference site, only the canonical binding of an unchanged signature stays a method group, and any other binding uses the lambda wrapper; an event subscription that needs a wrapper gets `DelegateEqualityRequired`. `PackedArguments` and `Proceed` are reserved for section [16.8](16-future-directions.md#168-the-proceed-and-packedarguments-sources-interception-without-a-template). | One interface for both kinds of result would offer existing methods members that change a signature, and positional binding would stay the only binding of existing methods. | M2 |
| PO63 | Canonical binding. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC64). A parameter that is not bound explicitly binds by name only: the receiver rules for the receiver, the argument of the same name for the other parameters, `CallerInfo` for a caller-information parameter, its default for an optional parameter of an existing method, and LAMA1013 `UnboundParameter` otherwise. `BindRemainingByPosition()` is the explicit opt-in, with identical types. | Positional binding by default swaps parameters of the same type silently, and depends on the parameter names of each overload. | M1 |
| PO64 | Added parameters. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC65). `IInterceptorBuilder.AddParameter( name, type, argument )` with a mandatory source and no default value; the value is site data; in a group with added parameters, `params` is packed and defaults are materialized; added arguments are appended as named arguments with unique names; `ExpressionFactory.Parse` is accepted for pulled values; no registration-level `IPullStrategy` overload in version 1; existing methods bind their trailing parameters with `Bind`. | Call-site data reaches a template only as a template argument, with one method per call site. | M2 |
| PO65 | Resolution of pulled parameters. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC66). A pulled parameter refers to the origin member only. The engine checks the name at the site and refuses a shadowed, non-capturable or statically enclosed parameter with LAMA1013 `PulledParameterNotAccessible`. `InterceptionContext.IsInNestedFunction` lets providers skip such sites. The parameters of enclosing lambdas are a future direction (section [16.11](16-future-directions.md#1611-functions-and-locals-that-enclose-a-site)). | Rule E16 alone accepts a shadowing lambda parameter of the same type, and the interceptor receives a wrong value silently. | M2 |
| PO66 | `AnyAwaitable`. | Decided by the product owner on 2026-09-25 (RC67). The open-source template types `AnyAwaitable` and `AnyAwaitable<T>` with their method builders, the milestone MA before M5, tracked by metalama/Metalama#919. For await interceptors, `async AnyAwaitable<dynamic?>` lets the engine choose the task type, and `async Task<dynamic?>` and `async ValueTask<dynamic?>` keep their literal meaning. | Await templates must name a concrete task type, and the usual `async Task<dynamic?>` gives `Task<R>` instead of the default `ValueTask<R>`. | M5 (MA before it) |
